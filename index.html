Before we go

    Museum embankmentThe Städel (Schaumainkai 63) and Museum Angewandte Kunst (Schaumainkai 17) are on the same riverside street, about 10 minutes apart. From the Adina, the Städel is about 20 minutes on foot over the Holbeinsteg footbridge.
    Ordering vegan and gluten freeChipotle: a bowl with rice, beans, fajita veggies, salsa and guac, no tortilla, cheese or sour cream. Im Herzen Afrikas: vegan platter with the corn injera. Olimpio: vegan spaghetti ice in a cup, no wafer.
    Getting aroundAny S-Bahn from the Hauptbahnhof goes to Hauptwache and Konstablerwache in a few minutes, handy for Chipotle, Pilates and Olimpio. Buy RMV tickets in the app.
