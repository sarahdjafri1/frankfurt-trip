Before we go

    Museum embankmentThe Städel (Schaumainkai 63) and Museum Angewandte Kunst (Schaumainkai 17) are on the same riverside street, about 10 minutes apart. U1, U2, U3 or U8 to Schweizer Platz. Both are closed Mondays.
    ChipotleGermany's only Chipotles are both in Frankfurt: MyZeil mall on the Zeil (until 21:00 Mon to Sat) and Skyline Plaza by the Messe. For vegan and gluten free, order a bowl: rice, beans, fajita veggies, salsa, guac. The flour tortilla has gluten, and cheese and sour cream aren't vegan.
    Lagree at SculptureHorst-Schulmann-Straße 3, next to the European Central Bank in the Ostend. 50 minute reformer classes, open weekdays 06:00 to 19:20. From the Adina it's a long walk, so take any S-Bahn from the Hauptbahnhof to Ostendstraße. Bring grip socks and arrive 10 to 15 minutes early for your first class.
